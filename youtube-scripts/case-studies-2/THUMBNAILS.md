# Case Studies 2 — Thumbnails, Titles, Descriptions & Tags

Art direction: **clickbaity + brand-forward.** Bold saturated brand-color backgrounds, the **official logo and brand name clearly visible**, and a short high-curiosity hook in heavy type. Instantly recognizable in the feed, engineered for clicks. (Titles, descriptions, tags below are final; the thumbnail is the hook.)

---

## SERIES THUMBNAIL SYSTEM (locked — keep every video consistent)

**The idea:** the brand is unmistakable in half a second, and the text creates a curiosity gap you have to resolve by clicking. Logo big and clear, bold claim, high contrast.

- **Background:** one bold, saturated brand color filling the frame (gradient, spotlight, or a dramatized brand object). High energy, high contrast, dramatic lighting. Leave clear empty space on one side for the text.
- **Logo / brand name (must be clearly visible):** place the **official brand logo or wordmark large** — top-center or beside the subject — so the brand reads instantly from a phone thumbnail. The brand name also appears in the text when it sharpens the hook.
- **Text overlay (the clickbait):** 2–5 words, a bold claim or curiosity gap. Heavy condensed display font (**Anton / Archivo Black / Impact**), white caps with a **punch color on the key word** (brand accent, or yellow/red), thin dark outline + drop shadow so it pops on any background. Big enough to read at small size.
- **Curiosity accent (optional, use sparingly):** one bold device — a giant number, a red circle, or an arrow — to spike intrigue. One per thumbnail, never a cluttered pile.
- **Consistency:** same font, same text treatment, logo clearly visible, same bold energy on all 10. A viewer should recognize it as one series and know the brand at a glance.

**How to build each one:**
1. Generate the **background only** with the prompt (no text, no logo — AI text/logos look broken).
2. In post: drop the **official logo** on, large and crisp.
3. Add the **bold caps hook** in the locked font with the key word in the punch color.

**Shared background prompt suffix (append to every per-video prompt):**
> bold saturated color background, dynamic high-contrast dramatic studio lighting, energetic and eye-catching, clean composition with generous empty space on one side for a text overlay, no text, no words, no letters, no logos, no watermark, ultra sharp, vibrant, 16:9, high resolution — YouTube thumbnail background.
