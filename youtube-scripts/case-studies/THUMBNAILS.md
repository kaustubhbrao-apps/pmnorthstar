# Case Studies — Thumbnails, Descriptions & Tags (v2: branded + clickbaity)

**Direction:** stylish but SCROLL-STOPPING. Every thumbnail = (1) a big, instantly
recognizable **brand logo**, (2) a **bold saturated brand-color** background (solid color
blocking, white/contrast type — the pmnorthstar house look), (3) a short **curiosity-gap
hook** that makes you need to click. Premium clickbait — no shocked faces, no messy arrows.

## Series thumbnail SYSTEM
- **Format:** 1280×720 (16:9).
- **Logo:** use the OFFICIAL brand logo (grab a clean SVG/PNG from the brand's press kit,
  Wikipedia, or brandfetch). Place it large and high-contrast. AI can't draw logos right —
  the prompts below make the *background/scene only*; you overlay the real logo + text.
- **Hook text:** 3–5 words, ALL CAPS or a bold display face, with ONE word emphasized in
  a contrasting color. Short enough to read in 0.5s on mobile.
- **Type:** one bold face for the whole series — **Archivo Black / Anton / Neue Montreal
  Bold** (modern, punchy). Keep it identical across all 10 for a cohesive channel.
- **Layout (fixed for all 10):** brand logo upper area or beside the subject · hook text
  lower two-thirds, large · small `pmnorthstar` wordmark fixed corner · optional thin
  bottom color bar in the brand accent.
- **Workflow:** generate the visual with AI (prompts say "no text, no logo"), then add the
  official logo + hook + wordmark in Canva/Figma so type and logo are razor-sharp.
- **Prompt suffix (append to every prompt):** `bold high-contrast, dramatic studio
  lighting, crisp, high detail, strong single focal point, saturated brand color, premium
  but eye-catching, no text, no logo, no watermark, no faces, 16:9 --ar 16:9 --style raw`
