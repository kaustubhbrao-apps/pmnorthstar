# Case Studies 3 — Titles, Thumbnails, Descriptions & Tags

Art direction: **clickbaity + brand-forward, logos baked INTO the image.** Bold saturated brand-color backgrounds, the **official logo / wordmark and brand elements rendered right in the thumbnail**, a short high-curiosity hook in heavy caps, one accent device. Covers all 15 videos (10 evergreen + 5 news specials).

---

## SERIES THUMBNAIL SYSTEM (locked)
- **Background:** one bold saturated brand color, dramatic lighting, with empty space on one side for the headline.
- **Logo + brand elements (IN the image):** each prompt renders the **official brand logo and wordmark, large and clearly visible** (top-center unless noted), plus the brand's signature iconography. The brand must be recognizable at a glance on mobile.
- **Hook text:** 2–5 words, heavy caps (**Anton / Archivo Black / Impact**), key word in a punch color (yellow/red/brand accent), dark outline + drop shadow.
- **One accent max:** a giant number, a red circle, or an arrow.
- **⚠️ Logo fallback:** AI models sometimes distort logos/letters. Generate with the logo in-image as written; if a render mangles the logo or wordmark, overlay the clean official logo in post. The big HEADLINE hook is still cleanest added in post (AI text is unreliable) — include it in the prompt only if the model handles text well.

**Shared prompt suffix (append to every prompt):**
> bold saturated color background, dynamic high-contrast dramatic studio lighting, energetic and eye-catching, clean composition with empty space on one side for a headline; render the brand logo and wordmark crisp, accurate and correctly spelled, no gibberish text, no watermark; ultra sharp, vibrant, 16:9, high resolution — YouTube thumbnail.

- **Link note:** descriptions point to the free library at **pmnorthstar.in** (specific `/case-study/<slug>` links aren't wired because these stories aren't all confirmed in the library yet — give me slugs and I'll swap exact links in).
