## A Broken TV and a 300% Price Hike

In 2010, Girish Mathrubootham was a senior director at Zoho in Chennai, India. That year, he ordered a new flat-screen television to be shipped from the United States to his home in India. The TV arrived completely shattered. 

For five agonizing months, Mathrubootham battled non-responsive customer service phone lines and unanswered emails trying to secure a refund. In frustration, he posted his experience on an online consumer forum called MouthShut. Within twenty-four hours, the shipping company called him, apologized, and delivered a full refund.

The experience made Mathrubootham acutely aware of the power of customer service. Months later, browsing Hacker News, he stumbled across a headline that set his entrepreneurial wheels in motion: **"Zendesk raises prices by 300% to 600%."**

The comments section was ablaze with fury from small business owners and startups whose monthly bills had quadrupled overnight. Mathrubootham realized that the leading player in customer support was leaving the bottom of the market completely defenseless. 

He quit his stable job, teamed up with Shan Krishnasamy, and launched **Freshdesk** from a modest office in Chennai.

## The Inbound SEO and Keyword Blitz

Freshdesk faced an immediate geographic handicap: how could a startup based in Chennai, India, sell business software to companies in California, London, and Sydney with zero brand recognition and a microscopic marketing budget?

Mathrubootham turned the geographic reality into an arbitrage weapon:
1. **Capitalizing on Zendesk Backlash:** When Zendesk increased prices, Freshdesk launched a cheeky, highly targeted campaign: *"RIP Zendesk. Welcome to Freshdesk."* They bid aggressively on Zendesk-related search terms on Google AdWords, capturing furious customers who were actively searching for alternatives.
2. **Programmatic Long-Tail SEO:** Freshdesk published hundreds of comprehensive guides, comparison pages, and templates for terms like *"free helpdesk software,"* *"customer service ticketing system,"* and *"Zendesk alternative."*
3. **Frictionless Free Trials:** While legacy enterprise software required "Contact Us for a Demo" forms and enterprise RFPs, Freshdesk allowed any company to sign up for a free 30-day trial with no credit card required.

## The Chennai Inside-Sales Machine

Once an overseas prospect signed up for a free trial, Freshworks deployed its secret operational weapon: **the Chennai-based 24/7 inside sales engine**.

Indian engineers and sales reps worked evening and overnight shifts in Chennai, perfectly matching the business hours of London and New York:
- The moment a trial user clicked around the dashboard, an account executive phoned them or sent a helpful in-app chat: *"Hey, I noticed you're trying to connect your email domain. Can I guide you over screen share right now?"*
- Because the operational cost structure in Chennai was a fraction of Silicon Valley salaries, Freshworks could afford to provide white-glove, instant customer onboarding to tiny $50/month customers who were completely ignored by US competitors.

Freshdesk converted trial users into paying subscribers at industry-leading velocity, accumulating thousands of customers across 100+ countries without an outside sales team ever boarding an airplane.

## Expanding to the Multi-Product Suite

Mathrubootham recognized that a standalone customer ticketing tool had a natural ceiling. Customer service agents didn't operate in a vacuum; they worked closely with IT support and sales teams.

Freshworks methodically executed a multi-product horizontal expansion:
- **Freshservice (2014):** An IT service management (ITSM) tool for internal employee tickets, directly competing with ServiceNow.
- **Freshsales (2016):** An intuitive CRM for sales teams, challenging Salesforce.
- **Freshchat (2017):** Modern conversational messaging and chatbots.

Renamed **Freshworks**, the company evolved into a unified business platform. Customers who started with Freshdesk for customer support quickly adopted Freshservice and Freshsales, driving massive expansion ARR.

In September 2021, Freshworks made history by ringing the opening bell on the Nasdaq in New York at a valuation of over $13 billion—the first Indian SaaS company to achieve a US public listing, and paving the way for the entire Indian SaaS ecosystem.

## Deep-Dive Takeaways for Builders

1. **Capitalize on Competitor Pricing Blunders:** When an incumbent abruptly increases prices or alters terms of service, they create an immediate wave of motivated buyers. Moving fast with targeted positioning can capture years of market share in a matter of weeks.
2. **Leverage Geographic Cost Arbitrage for Superior Service:** Having a lower structural cost base allows you to deliver high-touch onboarding, real-time support, and generous free tiers to SMB segments that expensive competitors cannot afford to serve.
3. **Plan the Multi-Product Suite from Day One:** Single-feature tools are vulnerable to platform bundling. Identifying adjacent workflows within the same customer organization allows you to expand contract value without increasing customer acquisition costs.
