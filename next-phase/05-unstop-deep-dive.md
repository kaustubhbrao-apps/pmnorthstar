## Unstop's Built-in Assessment Engine

Unstop has a robust **built-in** assessment and simulation engine (not just hosting third-party links).

### Proctoring in Practice
| Feature | How It Works |
|---------|-------------|
| **Identity Verification** | Mobile/email OTP + Aadhaar-based face registration + liveness detection (checks blinking/movements) |
| **Real-time Monitoring** | Continuous webcam tracking (flags multiple faces or looking away). Audio monitoring detects background noise/whispers |
| **Screen Security** | Enforced full-screen mode, tab-switching detection, copy-paste disabled |
| **Geo-tracking** | IP and geolocation tracking |
| **Audit Reports** | Automated time-stamped behavioral reports flagging suspicious activity (e.g., "candidate looked off-screen for 15s at 12:04") |

### Student Registration Flow
1. Register on unstop.com
2. Dashboard loads simulation environment natively (or via secure iframe)
3. Proctoring system initializes before start, requiring camera and mic permissions
4. Simulation begins

## Student Experiences (Reddit/Quora/Forums)

### What's Hard
> *"The time-pressure is the biggest hurdle. Market variables change dynamically, and you feel overwhelmed by the sheer number of levers you can pull while a countdown timer runs."*

### What's Annoying
> *"Looking down at scratch paper to do math can trigger a warning flag for 'looking away from screen.'"*
> *"Connectivity issues during a live, un-pausable simulation can completely derail a team."*

### How Students Prepare
1. **Practice on older simulation platforms** (Cesim, Markstrat) since mechanics are similar
2. **Build Excel models beforehand** to quickly plug in variables during the sim
3. **Research the sponsor company's annual reports** in advance
4. **Delegate dashboards** — one person watches competitor prices, one manages supply chain
5. **Divide into CXO roles** rather than everyone looking at the same screen
