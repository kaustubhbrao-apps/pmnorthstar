## B-School Fest Events

### IIM Ahmedabad — The Red Brick Summit
- **Format:** 3-stage: Round 1 (Online quiz/teaser video) → Round 2 (Case submission, PPT) → Round 3 (CXO Finale presentation)
- **Team Size:** 2-3 members

### IIM Bangalore — Vista / Eximius
- **Format:**
  1. Online preliminary screening (Quiz on Unstop)
  2. Case Submission (PPT or detailed report)
  3. Grand Finale (8-10 min presentation + 4 min Q&A, offline/hybrid)
- **Note:** Some tracks include a Mentorship round before finals

### IIM Calcutta — Intaglio
- **Format:** Round 1 (Online elimination/Whitepaper) → Round 2 (Semi-final/Video pitch) → Finals (On-campus jury presentation)

### IIM Lucknow — Manfest-Varchasva
- **Format:**
  1. 2-5 slide initial approach deck
  2. Detailed case analysis submission (PDF/PPT)
  3. Final Presentation (15 min + 5 min Q&A, virtual or on-campus)

### ISB — Advaita
- Standard 3-round format managed on Unstop

### XLRI — Ensemble (Circus Maximus)
- **Format:** Case submissions require PPTX/PDF (minimum 5 slides excluding appendices)
- **Must cover:** Problem, Analysis, Strategy, and Implementation

### SPJIMR — Ojas
- **Format:** Multiple track-based events (Finance, Strategy, PM)
  1. Online quiz/assessment
  2. 2-3 slide preliminary case submission
  3. National Finals (8+ slides, formal pitch)
- **Special:** Includes **"surprise" or "exigency" rounds** to test crisis management

### MDI Gurgaon — Imperium
- **Format:**
  1. Online quiz
  2. 2-3 slide Executive Summary (strict naming conventions)
  3. Grand Finale (10 min presentation + 5 min Q&A)

### FMS Delhi — Finale
- **Format:** High-stakes on-campus pitches
  - 7-12 slides (plus appendix for models/data)
  - **8-10 min pitch, 5-7 min Q&A** (strict time limits)
  - Judges look for real-time crisis handling
  - **Drastic changes from earlier round submissions are penalized**

### IIFT — Asian Paints Alchemy
- **Format:** Coatings/materials innovation focus
  1. 3-10 slide PPT (data/Excel must be embedded)
  2. Refined 10-slide PPT responding to specific jury questions

### NMIMS — SBM Events
- **Format:**
  1. 20-min online quiz
  2. 2-5 slide initial case solution
  3. Final pitch

## Standard Competition Format (Common Across All)

```
Round 1: Online Quiz/Assessment (15-30 min)
  ↓ top 50% advance
Round 2: Case Submission (1-5 day window)
  - Receive 2-5 page case study/brief
  - Submit 3-8 slide deck or executive summary PDF
  ↓ top 10-20 teams advance
Round 3: Semi-Finals
  - 7-15 min presentation + 3-10 min Q&A
  ↓ top 4-8 teams advance
Round 4: Grand Finale
  - 5-10 min presentation + 10-15 min deep-dive Q&A
  - Often at company headquarters
  - Judged by senior leadership / CXOs
```

## Key Observations for northstar

1. **The "3-slide deck" format is universal** — students need to practice distilling complex analysis into 3 slides
2. **Q&A pressure is the differentiator** — finals are won/lost in the Q&A, not the presentation
3. **"Exigency rounds" at SPJIMR** — surprise crisis rounds test adaptability (perfect for northstar's mid-sim shocks)
4. **Feasibility > Creativity** — every competition values executable roadmaps over flashy ideas
5. **PPIs are the real prize** — competitions are career pipelines, not just trophies
6. **All registrations flow through Unstop** — northstar should position as the practice complement to Unstop
