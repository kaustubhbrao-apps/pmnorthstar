# PM Interview Prep Platform Research
## Exponent · RocketBlocks · Product Alliance · Product Sandbox · ProductDo · Pramp

*Compiled August 10, 2026*

---

## 1. Exponent (tryexponent.com)

### How It Works
- Structured courses (role/company specific) + large database of crowd-sourced interview questions
- Practice via **peer-to-peer mock interviews** or **AI-powered interview practice**

### Question/Scenario Types
Product sense, strategy, execution, analytics, behavioral

### Feedback
- **AI:** Instant feedback on practice answers
- **Peer:** Human feedback via scheduled mock interviews
- **Expert:** Paid 1:1 coaching (extra cost)

### Pricing
~$12/month (paid annually) for unlimited peer mocks and course content. Free tier has limited access.

### User Reviews
- ✅ Centralized "all-in-one" platform, great structure, good for finding peers
- ❌ Expensive vs free alternatives, video/peer quality inconsistent

### vs. northstar
Exponent is broad (courses + peer matching). SimulateIt is focused on **interactive branching decisions with consequences**. Exponent's AI is conversational; SimulateIt's is scenario-based gamification.

---

## 2. RocketBlocks (rocketblocks.me)

### How It Works
A **"gym for your brain."** Self-paced, skill-based micro-drills rather than full mock interviews.

### Question/Scenario Types
- Product sense
- Strategy
- Analytics (chart reading, mental math)
- Execution

### Feedback
System-based feedback (drills/concept reviews) + self-calibration against expert answers. 1:1 coaching available ($200/hour).

### Pricing
~$155/year for full access

### User Reviews
- ✅ Component-based drills highly praised for **isolating weaknesses**. High-quality content. Efficient (5-15 mins per drill).
- ❌ Doesn't replace live human mock interviews

### vs. northstar
Very aligned. RocketBlocks uses short, targeted drills — SimulateIt could adopt their **component-based categorization** (by skill: metrics, strategy, product sense).

## 3. Product Alliance

### How It Works
Premium video-based courses focused on **company-specific deep dives** (FAANG) and interview strategies.

### Pricing
$400-600+ for full access bundles (sometimes discounted to ~$370)

### User Reviews
- ✅ Highly tailored to FAANG, "candidate-led" interview style
- ❌ Very expensive; foundational knowledge available free elsewhere

### vs. northstar
Traditional high-ticket infoproduct. northstar is free, interactive, indie. northstar wins on accessibility and gamified practice.

---

## 4. The Product Sandbox

### How It Works
**Adversarial AI simulations** that "push back" on user answers. Acts as a **"flight simulator"** for PMs.

### Question/Scenario Types
Multi-turn adversarial mock interviews, hostile stakeholders, product trade-offs, system design

### Feedback
Purely adversarial AI designed to test judgment and EQ under stress. Immediate scored feedback.

### Pricing
Premium/bespoke (individual + enterprise hiring assessments)

### vs. northstar
**The closest conceptual cousin to SimulateIt.** While SimulateIt uses branching narratives with predefined consequences, Product Sandbox uses generative AI to dynamically argue. SimulateIt is curated + static; Sandbox is dynamic + adversarial.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Product Sandbox proves there's appetite for "adversarial" pushback. SimulateIt could introduce **"hostile stakeholder" branches** where picking the "safe" framework answer leads to bad outcomes, rewarding adaptive reasoning.

## 5. ProductDo

### How It Works
**Job simulations** instead of video courses. "Learning-by-doing" with realistic business cases.

### Question/Scenario Types
Feature prioritization, product discovery, writing SQL, analytics, A/B testing. Uses real-world tools.

### Feedback
Simulator feedback benchmarked against senior PMs

### Pricing
Single-course access with lifetime updates

### vs. northstar
Similar hands-on philosophy. ProductDo focuses on **hard technical skills** (SQL, APIs); SimulateIt focuses on **business/product decisions**.

---

## 6. Pramp / Interviewing.io

### Pramp (now part of Exponent)
Free peer-to-peer mock interviews. Great for high-volume practice. Marketplace model.

### Interviewing.io
Focused almost exclusively on engineering/technical mock interviews with FAANG engineers. Weak on PM tracks.

### vs. northstar
Pramp is a peer marketplace; northstar is a **single-player curated experience**.

---

## 7. igotanoffer.com

### How It Works
Extensive free written guides + marketplace for paid 1-on-1 coaching

### Pricing
Guides are free. Coaching: $100-250/hour via credit system.

### vs. northstar
Reading material + service business. northstar is a **product library + interactive tools**.

## Market Trends (2025-2026)

1. **AI-Native PM interviews** — now test for AI fluency (designing GenAI features, understanding inference costs)
2. **AI Mock Tools emerging** — PMMockr, LockedIn AI, MockJam becoming standard for volume practice
3. **Shift from frameworks to reasoning** — interviewers want adaptive thinking, not robotic CIRCLES/STAR recitation

## Key Takeaways for northstar

1. **SimulateIt's format is validated** — GoPractice charges $1,690 for essentially the same "RPG choose-your-adventure" format
2. **Adopt RocketBlocks' categorization** — tag drills by skill (Product Sense, Execution, Strategy, Analytics)
3. **Add Product Sandbox-style adversarial branches** — "hostile stakeholder" scenarios where safe answers fail
4. **Free is the moat** — every competitor charges $12-$1,690. Being free + high-quality = unstoppable for student adoption
5. **AI mock tools are the future** — northstar AI mentor + DebateIt position us there
