## The Vision

Three pillars:
1. **SimulateIt Pro** — Business simulations for MBA case comp practice (Sprint / Full / Marathon formats)
2. **Tools Suite** — CheckIt + DebateIt + AuditIt
3. **northstar AI** — Conversational PM mentor grounded in 143 case studies

**Target:** MBA students in India (IIMs, ISB, XLRI, SPJIMR, 5,000+ programs)

**Positioning:** Free + real companies + India-first. No competitor offers all three.

**The gap we fill:** Students told us *"there's no safe practice ground"* — they start with small college fests because there's nowhere to practice. northstar is that practice ground.
