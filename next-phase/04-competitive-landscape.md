## 1. Unstop (unstop.com — formerly Dare2Compete)

*India's dominant talent engagement, hackathon, and assessment platform.*

### Assessment & Simulation Structure
- **Multi-Stage Challenges:** Structured in rounds (e.g., Round 1: Timed MCQ Quiz → Round 2: Executive Summary/PPT Submission → Round 3: Prototype/Business Case → Round 4: Live Pitch).
- **Diverse Question Types:** MCQs, coding sprints (multi-language compilers), situational judgment tests (SJTs), and whiteboarding/business plan uploads.

### Proctoring Capabilities (360° AI Integrity)
- **Identity Verification:** Aadhaar/Government ID verification, face match, and movement/liveness detection.
- **Real-time Monitoring:** Multi-face detection, unauthorized device detection, ambient sound/voice tracking.
- **Browser & OS Lockdown:** Full-screen enforcement, tab-switch detection, IP & geolocation tracking.
- **Audit Logging:** Automated evidence reports featuring timestamped video clips of flagged infractions.

### Scoring & Feedback
- Automated instant scoring for quizzes/coding.
- Human evaluation rubrics for case study submissions.
- Recruiter scorecards with section-wise percentiles and predictive hiring scores.

### Certifications
- Verified certificates of participation, finalist achievements, and co-branded corporate credentials (e.g., Tata, Reliance, Flipkart challenges).

### Business Model
- **B2B Heavy** (SaaS licenses for campus recruitment, corporate hackathons, employer branding) + **B2C** talent discovery marketplace.

### Gamification & Retention
- Live competition leaderboards (College vs. National level).
- Public portfolio profile showcasing rank, badges, and challenge wins.
- Alert notifications for recurring annual flagship corporate competitions.

## 2. HackerRank & HackerEarth

*Standard-bearers for developer screening and proctored technical evaluations.*

### Assessment Structure
- Timed coding challenges, SQL data queries, system design scenarios, full-stack project building, and algorithmic problem sets.
- **HackerEarth:** Native support for hosting enterprise hackathons and custom project-based assessments.

### Proctoring Capabilities
- **HackerRank:** 3-tier integrity stack (Secure Mode browser lock, Proctor Mode camera/mic AI, and OS-level Desktop lockdown). AI plagiarism engine comparing code against MOSS databases and internet sources.
- **HackerEarth:** "Smart Browser" tech, webcam snapshots, eye-tracking, VM & screen-sharing detection, and anti-GenAI leakage detection.

### Scoring & Feedback
- Automated unit test execution testing accuracy, time complexity, and memory usage.
- **HackerEarth Code Quality (SonarQube):** Evaluates maintainability, complexity, duplicates, and security vulnerabilities.
- **Codeplayer:** Key-by-key session replay recording the candidate's exact typing and debugging process.

### Certifications
- HackerRank Verified Role/Skill Certificates (SQL Advanced, Problem Solving, React, Node.js) with shareable LinkedIn badges.

## 3. Brilliant.org

*The benchmark for tactile, active visual learning and daily habit-building.*

### Learning Structure
- **Active Learning ("Learn by Doing"):** Zero passive long video lectures. Interactive visual widgets and puzzles.
- **Micro-Learning:** 15-minute bite-sized lessons focused on first-principles problem-solving.

### Scoring & Feedback
- Immediate tactile feedback upon manipulating visual widgets.
- **AI Tutor ("Koji"):** Provides progressive Socratic hints — guiding the learner without giving the direct answer.

### Gamification & Retention
- **Daily Streaks:** Prominent streak counter with streak repair/freeze mechanics (high loss-aversion hook).
- XP/Points, Level progression, Badges, and Daily Challenge progress rings.
- Optimized push notifications for daily 15-minute micro-habits.

### Business Model
- **B2C Subscription** (Monthly/Annual) + **B2B for Education** (Teacher & Parent progress dashboards).

## 4. Case & PM Interview Platforms: RocketBlocks, CaseCoach, Exponent

### Assessment Structure
- **RocketBlocks:** Short (5-10 min) skill-focused drills: Product Sense, Metric Drops, Technical APIs, Market Estimation, Pricing/GTM.
- **CaseCoach & StrategyCase:** Framework video courses, structured case drills, AI-assisted voice practice, and peer-to-peer live mock rooms.
- **Exponent:** Comprehensive question banks with video answers, AI mock practice portal, and 1-on-1 expert coaching.

### Scoring & Feedback
- **Self-Grading Rubrics:** Structured rubrics comparing user response against model answers.
- **Peer Scorecards:** Quantitative scoring across structure, depth, communication, and business logic.
- **AI Practice Feedback:** NLP evaluating answer completeness.

### Business Model
- **B2C Subscription / One-time Courses** + **B2B Institutional Licenses** (University business schools, PM bootcamps).
- **Pricing:** RocketBlocks/Exponent: $149-299/month.

## 5. Dedicated PM Simulators: GoPractice, Product Sandbox, ProductDo

### GoPractice Simulator
- **Mechanism:** Learner assumes the role of a PM inside a simulated company facing real business situations.
- **Data Integration:** Learner works directly with realistic data analytics dashboards, runs SQL queries, tests hypotheses, designs A/B experiments, and evaluates unit economics/retention curves.
- **Feedback Loop:** Decisions branch into simulated business outcomes (metric lifts vs. churn spikes), followed by deep analytical debriefs.

### The Product Sandbox
- Adversarial AI simulations that challenge candidate product hypotheses under constraints and push back on user assumptions.

### ProductDo
- Interactive simulators for hard PM skills (analytics, APIs, AI/LLM integration).

### Pre-Employment Screening (TestGorilla / CodeSignal / Mercer Mettl / Vervoe)
- Standardized pre-employment PM tests evaluating product strategy, agile methods, data interpretation, and situational judgment.
